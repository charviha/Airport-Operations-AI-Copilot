# LAX Pricing Policy

## 1. Standard Pricing

The standard surge multiplier at Los Angeles International Airport (LAX) is 1.0x.

Surge pricing may be used when request demand materially exceeds available driver supply.

## 2. Surge Limits

The maximum permitted surge multiplier at LAX is 1.4x.

A surge multiplier from 1.0x through 1.2x may be applied when operational demand conditions justify an increase.

A surge increase above 1.2x requires approval from the designated airport operations manager.

A multiplier above 1.4x is not permitted under the standard LAX pricing policy.

## 3. Conditions for Surge

Before increasing surge, the operations team should review:

- Completion rate
- Request volume
- Active driver count
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Average ETA
- Current surge multiplier

## 4. Approval Requirement

Any proposed surge increase above 1.2x requires human approval.

The approval request should include:

- Current surge multiplier
- Proposed surge multiplier
- Completion rate
- Request volume
- Active driver count
- Reason for the increase

## 5. Policy Restrictions

The surge multiplier must never exceed 1.4x.

The AI system must not recommend or execute a surge multiplier above the LAX maximum.

If the requested surge violates the policy, the action must be blocked.

## 6. Monitoring

After a surge change, the operations team should monitor completion rate, ETA, request volume, active drivers, queue size, and cancellation rate.
