# SFO Pricing Policy

## 1. Standard Pricing

The standard surge multiplier at San Francisco International Airport (SFO) is 1.0x.

Surge pricing may be used when demand significantly exceeds available driver supply.

## 2. Surge Limits

The maximum permitted surge multiplier at SFO is 1.5x.

A surge multiplier from 1.0x through 1.3x may be applied when operational conditions justify an increase.

A surge increase above 1.3x requires approval from the designated airport operations manager.

A surge multiplier above 1.5x is not permitted under the standard SFO pricing policy.

## 3. Conditions for Surge

Before increasing surge, the operations team should review:

- Completion rate
- Request volume
- Active driver count
- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Average ETA
- Current surge multiplier

If completion rate falls below 85% while demand remains elevated, the operations team may consider increasing surge within the permitted limit.

## 4. Approval Requirement

Any surge increase above 1.3x requires human approval.

The approval request must include:

- Current surge multiplier
- Proposed surge multiplier
- Completion rate
- Request volume
- Active driver count
- Reason for the increase

## 5. Policy Restrictions

The surge multiplier must never exceed 1.5x.

The AI system must not recommend or execute a surge multiplier above the policy maximum.

If a proposed multiplier violates the policy, the action must be blocked.

## 6. Monitoring After Surge Change

After a surge adjustment, the operations team should monitor:

- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Request volume
- Active drivers
- Driver cancellation rate

Further changes should be based on operational evidence and applicable approval requirements.
