# SFO Airport Operations Policy

## 1. Completion Rate

The expected airport trip completion rate at SFO is above 85%.

A completion rate below 85% should be treated as an operational warning.

A completion rate below 75% should be treated as a critical operational condition.

The operations team should investigate the cause of low completion rates before taking corrective action.

## 2. Driver Queue Management

The SFO airport driver queue should be monitored continuously.

A queue size above 150 drivers indicates elevated supply congestion.

A queue size above 200 drivers requires investigation by the airport operations team.

Drivers are expected to follow the designated airport queue process.

## 3. Average ETA

The target average pickup ETA at SFO is below 15 minutes.

An average ETA above 15 minutes should be investigated.

An average ETA above 20 minutes represents a severe operational condition.

## 4. Operational Investigation

When the completion rate falls below the expected threshold, the operations team should investigate:

- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Average ETA
- Active driver supply
- Request volume
- Current surge multiplier

## 5. Corrective Actions

Possible corrective actions include:

- Increasing driver supply through incentives
- Adjusting surge pricing within policy limits
- Monitoring driver cancellations
- Monitoring queue conditions
- Reviewing request volume

All corrective actions must comply with the applicable SFO pricing and driver policies.

## 6. Escalation

Critical operational conditions should be escalated to the airport operations manager.

The AI system may identify and recommend corrective actions but must follow applicable approval and policy requirements before executing high-risk actions.
