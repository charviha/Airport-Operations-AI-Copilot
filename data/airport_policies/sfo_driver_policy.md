# SFO Driver Policy

## 1. Airport Queue

Drivers entering the SFO airport queue are expected to remain in the designated queue until they receive an eligible trip or leave through the approved exit process.

Drivers must follow airport queue procedures.

## 2. Queue Abandonment

Drivers must not intentionally abandon the airport queue after receiving a trip assignment.

Repeated unauthorized queue abandonment may result in driver review.

The operations team should monitor unusual queue-abandonment and cancellation patterns.

## 3. Driver Cancellations

Drivers should avoid unnecessary cancellations after accepting an airport trip.

A high driver cancellation rate should be investigated as part of airport operational monitoring.

## 4. Driver Incentives

Driver incentives may be considered when available driver supply is insufficient to meet airport demand.

The incentive amount should be determined based on:

- Operational severity
- Driver supply shortage
- Request volume
- Expected duration of the shortage
- Available incentive budget

## 5. Incentive Approval

Normal driver incentives may be calculated by the operations system.

High-value incentives require the applicable human approval before execution.

The AI system must not bypass required approval.

## 6. Compliance

All driver incentives and operational actions must comply with SFO airport pricing and operations policies.

Policy violations must be blocked and escalated to the appropriate operations manager.
