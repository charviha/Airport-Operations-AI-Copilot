# JFK Airport Operations Policy

## 1. Completion Rate

The expected airport trip completion rate at John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK) is above 86%.

A completion rate below 86% should trigger an operational review.

A completion rate below 76% should be treated as a critical operational condition.

## 2. Driver Queue Management

The JFK driver queue should be monitored continuously.

A queue size above 140 drivers indicates elevated supply congestion.

A queue size above 200 drivers requires investigation by the airport operations team.

Drivers are expected to follow the designated JFK airport queue process.

## 3. Average ETA

The target average pickup ETA at JFK is below 14 minutes.

An average ETA above 14 minutes should be investigated.

An average ETA above 20 minutes represents a severe operational condition.

## 4. Operational Investigation

When completion rate falls below the expected threshold, the operations team should investigate:

- Driver cancellation rate
- Queue size
- Average ETA
- Active driver supply
- Request volume
- Current surge multiplier

## 5. Corrective Actions

Possible corrective actions include:

- Increasing driver supply through incentives
- Adjusting surge pricing within JFK policy limits
- Monitoring driver cancellations
- Monitoring queue conditions
- Reviewing request volume

All corrective actions must comply with JFK pricing and driver policies.

## 6. Escalation

Critical operational conditions should be escalated to the airport operations manager.

High-impact operational actions require the appropriate approval before execution.
